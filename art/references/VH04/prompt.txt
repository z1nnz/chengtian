The building: "VH04 street shop house" (臨街店屋) in a southern Chinese mountain village. NOT Japanese.
- Overall 7 m wide (along the street), 6 m deep including the eaves and awning, 6.5 m to the top of the ridge.
- One tall storey (wall plates at about 3.2 m) with a low storage loft under the roof; a gable roof whose ridge runs ALONG the street, so the long eave faces the street and the two triangular gables are at the left and right sides. Roof: dark grey pan-and-roll tiles, a gently concave curve, a stacked tile ridge with small, low upturned ends, moss on the tiles. Eave overhangs about 0.9 m at the front.
- Street front: the ground floor is completely OPEN between four square dark timber posts (three bays). A heavy timber beam over the posts carries a narrow strip of timber boards up to the eave. The removable vertical shop-front boards (narrow planks, about 2.4 m tall) are taken down and stacked leaning against the wall at one end of the front.
- Inside the open front: a low timber counter (about 0.9 m high) across the middle bay; wooden shelves with three or four tiers along the back and side walls holding jars, baskets and cloth bundles; an earth or plank floor raised one step (0.3 m) above the street on a stone edge.
- A faded cloth awning (beige / pale ochre canvas with a darker patch sewn on) stretched out from under the front eave and held up by two thin wooden poles at its outer corners, sagging a little between them.
- Sides (gables): white lime plaster with grey stains and repairs over a low rubble-stone base, dark timber corner posts and a middle rail, one small lattice window high in each gable for the loft, a small lattice window lower down.
- Back: plaster wall, a plank back door, one small lattice window.
