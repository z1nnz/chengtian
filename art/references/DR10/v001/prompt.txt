These sheets are for building a real-time 3D game model, so every feature must be a BIG, CLEAR, READABLE SHAPE that a modeller can copy, not fine painterly noise. Keep the silhouette simple and strong.

The asset: "DR10 dead standing trees (snags)", TWO kinds of tall dead trees still standing in the forest, with NO leaves at all:
- Kind A: about 8 m tall, trunk about 0.7 m diameter at the base. The top is snapped off at about 8 m with a jagged, splintered break (long pale splinters pointing up on one side). Two or three short broken-off branch stubs and one longer dead side branch bending up. Root flare at the base spreading into 4-5 short roots gripping the ground.
- Kind B: about 14 m tall, trunk about 1.0 m diameter at the base, slightly leaning and gently curved. Still has a few long bare dead branches in the upper half (twisted, pointing up and out, ending in broken tips) giving a striking, graceful silhouette, like a skeleton tree. The very top is a thin broken spike. Root flare with 5-6 roots.
Both:
- Large patches where the bark has peeled away, showing smooth pale silver-grey weathered wood underneath with long vertical cracks; the remaining bark is deep grey-brown with vertical furrows; the edges of the peeled patches curl slightly.
- Two or three round woodpecker holes (dark, about 8-12 cm) in a vertical row on the trunk, and some shelf fungus (bracket fungi) on the lower trunk.
- Dark olive-green moss only near the base and on the upward-facing sides of the roots; pale grey-green lichen spots higher up.
- Ground: a little leaf litter and moss around the root flare.
