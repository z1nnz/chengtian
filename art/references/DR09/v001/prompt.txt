The asset: "DR09 piles of broken branches", THREE sizes of a loose heap of dead fallen branches lying on the forest floor, the kind that collects under a giant tree after a storm:
- Size S: about 1.5 m long, 1.0 m wide, 0.4 m tall. 5 to 7 branches, 4 to 10 cm thick, crossing each other in an X / lattice, one forked branch.
- Size M: about 2.5 m long, 1.6 m wide, 0.7 m tall. 8 to 12 branches, 5 to 18 cm thick, one thicker main limb lying underneath, smaller ones leaning across it, a few ends propped up off the ground.
- Size L: about 4 m long, 2.2 m wide, 1.1 m tall. 12 to 18 branches, 5 to 28 cm thick, one big broken limb (about 28 cm) as the base with side forks, branches stacked on it at different angles, some ends sticking up diagonally.
All three:
- Branches are dead but not rotten mush: grey-brown bark, gently bent and knobbly with short broken side twigs (stubs), never straight poles or cylinders of equal thickness; each tapers towards the tip.
- Broken ends: torn, splintered breaks with pale warm-cream wood fibres and a few long jagged splinters on one side, not clean saw cuts. Some ends with bark peeled back showing pale wood.
- Patches of dark olive-green moss on the upward-facing sides of the thicker branches only, pale grey-green lichen spots, a few small tan mushrooms on the thickest limb.
- Brown and orange-brown dead leaves trapped between the branches and piled underneath, plus loose twigs and leaf litter on the ground around the base, so the pile sits on the ground and does not float.
- Clear gaps between branches so you can see through the pile in places.
