Three-view turnaround sheet of an old ginkgo tree (Ginkgo biloba) in autumn, about 16 m tall. Tall and straight with a single upright leader, grey furrowed bark; short limbs leave the trunk at a steep upward angle in uneven tiers, so the crown is tall and narrow-conical but irregular, with gaps between tiers and clusters. Small fan-shaped leaves grow in clusters on short spur shoots along every branch, following the twig flow. Old muted gold: dark gold, ochre and yellow-brown, with some leaves still dull olive-green inside; never lemon yellow, never orange neon.
Foliage method (most important): the crown is built from thousands of small individual solid leaves (or needle tufts) that follow curving twig-tip flows; three levels of thin branches (limb, secondary, fine twig) visibly pass in front of and behind the leaves; the crown has holes of different sizes where sky and branches show through; front and back layers of foliage give depth. Never round balls, never cloud puffs, never flat leaf cards, never a lollipop or an ellipsoid crown, never mirrored left-right branches. Art direction: stylized hand-painted semi-realistic 3D game art for an Eastern xianxia world set in a southern Chinese mountain village valley, calm natural colours (never neon green, never lemon yellow), soft blue-violet shadows, painterly large soft gradients, rich detail with a clear readable silhouette. Not Japanese style, not bonsai.
Layout: front view, right side view and back view side by side in one wide image, all three at the same scale and aligned on the same ground line, orthographic (no perspective), the whole tree visible with margin around it, plain light warm-grey background, even soft studio light, no cast shadow on the background, one tree only, no ground patch, no rocks, no grass, no pot, no people, no text, no labels.
